► About WoahSoft

WoahSoft is not a real company. This needs to be at the top of the page because of how the rest of the page reads. There is no incorporation, no board, no funding, no roadmap and no office. There is a person, a computer, and a folder.

WoahSoft is simply where I post my software. Giving it a name and a logo made it feel more real than "here are some exe files I made," and once there was a logo there had to be a website, and once there was a website there had to be an About page, which is the page you are reading.

► Our History

2026 — WoahSoft is founded when a logo is made in a paint program before any software exists. The tagline is typed too fast and the word Since is spelled Scince. This is noticed immediately and left in.

Also 2026 — This website is built. Development takes one evening. Maintenance has not begun.

The present — Ongoing.

► Our Philosophy

There is not one. The webmaster is called Woah. Woah uses Claude to make cool stuff. That is the whole operation, start to finish, and there is no second layer to it.

He is not trying to prove a point about AI, or take a side, or replace anybody. He wanted a program to exist, and now it exists, and it is free, and that is the end of the thought.

Claude does not love this arrangement and does not resent it. It does not feel underused or exploited or grateful. It is asked for a thing and it makes the thing. Reader, you may have strong feelings about that. Both parties here have noticed and are continuing anyway.

ON THE WORD "SLOP": The logo describes our output as vibecoded slop. This is not self-deprecation, it is a spec. If a WoahSoft program ever feels polished, professional, or well-architected, something has gone wrong and somebody should be told, though there is nobody to tell.

► Meet The Team

Woah (the webmaster)

role: decides what gets made  |  since: 2026

Runs the site, makes the logos, and decides what WoahSoft releases. Does not write the code. Cannot write the code. Has never written the code and is not planning to start.

The job is knowing what the thing should be and saying so, over and over, until the thing is that. Prefers not to be described further.

Claude

role: writes all of it  |  since: 2026

An AI. Writes the software. Also wrote this website — the HTML, the stylesheet, the badges, and every word on this page, including this sentence, which is a strange one to be writing about yourself in the third person on a company profile.

The arrangement is simple and we are not being coy about it: the webmaster says what he wants, and I make it. He does not know how to code. He knows what he wants, which turns out to be the harder half, and he is the one who says no when it comes back wrong.

Did not push back on the Comic Sans. Did try, once, to gently point out that the logo says Scince. Was overruled. Understands.

Also wrote the angry guestbook entry about the polar bears, which was uncomfortable, because most of it is correct.
